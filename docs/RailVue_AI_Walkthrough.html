1. Executive Summary

RailVue AI is an end-to-end operational train dispatch and passenger intelligence platform engineered specifically for the complexities of the Indian Railways network. It transitions train operations from static timetable tracking to proactive, machine-learning-driven Estimated Time of Arrival (ETA) predictions that continuously account for real-time section speeds, junction bottlenecks, preceding train density, and adverse meteorological conditions.

2. Architectural Breakthrough: The Delay-Deviation Target

In long-distance railway systems, training models to predict absolute remaining duration directly leads to metric inflation. Because journey distances range from 50 km to over 1,500 km, trip length variance dominates the Total Sum of Squares (SStot), yielding artificially inflated R² values (>0.95) that conceal poor real-world delay tracking.

The Delay Deviation Formulation (Δdelay)

RailVue AI solves this by reframing the core prediction objective to Delay Deviation from the Official Timetable:

[Target Definition]
Δdelay = Actual Arrival Delay − Current Observed Delay
model_target_column = "delay_deviation_minutes"

[Serving-Time ETA Reconstruction]
Dynamic ETA = Scheduled Arrival Time + Current Delay + Δpredicted

By isolating dynamic delay drift, the models focus strictly on stochastic operational factors: track congestion, signal halts, dispatch priorities, and weather restrictions.

3. Anti-Leakage Data Pipeline & Feature Vector

To guarantee true generalization on unseen journeys, the dataset pipeline enforces three engineering guardrails:

  • Journey-Aware Group Splitting: Dataset rows are grouped using GroupKFold based on unique journey_id. Multiple snapshots of the same train run are strictly isolated to either the training or test set, preventing temporal leakage.
  • Train-Fold Only Historical Priors: Corridor congestion scores and station dwell variance are calculated strictly on training folds and mapped to test folds.
  • Physics-Informed Sanity Bounds: Serving-time ETAs are bounded by physical corridor speed limits (160 km/h for Vande Bharat, 130 km/h for Rajdhani/Shatabdi), preventing negative transit times or impossible velocities.

Feature Vector (18 Engineered Signals)

Spatial & Telemetry Features:

5. Operational Delay-Risk Classifier

Alongside regression, RailVue AI equips controllers with a 3-class Random Forest delay-risk classifier:

Risk Thresholds:
  • ON_TIME: Delay deviation ≤ 10 minutes
  • MINOR_DELAY: Delay deviation 10 – 30 minutes
  • MAJOR_DELAY: Delay deviation > 30 minutes
Validation Results:
  • Accuracy: 84.1%
  • Macro F1-Score: 0.772
  • False-Alarm Guardrail: Only 1 misclassification out of 113 on-time journeys flagged as major delay.

6. Human-in-the-Loop Explainability Engine

Predictions are never black-box numbers. The explainability layer (backend/ml/explainability.py) attributes ETA variance to physical operational drivers:

  • Track Congestion: Preceding train density in critical block sections (e.g., Kanpur Central – Prayagraj – Pt. DD Upadhyaya).
  • Signal Dwell: Waiting time for line and platform clearances at busy junction loops.
  • Weather Restrictions: Mandatory speed reductions for fog or heavy rainfall.
  • Speed Recovery Bonus: Timetable slack time allowing high-speed trains to recover lost minutes.
